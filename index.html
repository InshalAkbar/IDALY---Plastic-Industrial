Our Services

What We Are Offering

Plastic Bottles

Idaly supplies a wide range plastic bottles en PET bottles for various B2B applications. Whether you are looking for transparent PET bottles, sturdy HDPE bottles or sustainable options from recycled R-PETWith us, you'll always find the right packaging for your product. Available in clear, amber, and white, in sizes from 30 ml to 1 liter and larger.

Plastic Buckets

Idaly supplies a wide range of plastic buckets for various B2B applications. Whether you are looking for sturdy HDPE buckets, lightweight LDPE buckets or sustainable options from recycled R-PET, we have the right packaging for your needs. Available in clear, amber, and white, in sizes from 1 liter to 20 liters and larger.

Plastic Pots

Idaly supplies a wide range of plastic pots for various B2B applications. Whether you are looking for sturdy HDPE pots, lightweight LDPE pots or sustainable options from recycled R-PET, we have the right packaging for your needs. Available in clear, amber, and white, in sizes from 1 liter to 20 liters and larger.
